Privacy

Echoes is a free hobby project. It has no ads, no cookies and no tracking. It collects an anonymous summary of each finished round, so everyone can see what others guessed. An account is optional – only if you sign in do we store your e-mail address and your results.

Who is responsible

Tim Pröchel · contact via GitHub: github.com/timprchl

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

What is stored on your device

Your progress for the day, your streak and which tips you have already seen are saved in your browser’s local storage. Without an account this data never leaves your device. If you sign in, it is also saved to your account (see below), and a sign-in key is kept in your browser. You can delete the local data at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Anonymous game statistics

When you finish a round, the game sends a short, anonymous summary to our statistics service: the day, how many voices you heard, on which guess you found it, your points, whether you named the event, and for each guess only the country and the decade. To count each device once per day it uses a random ID created in your browser – no name, no account, no IP address is stored. These summaries are shown to all players as percentages on the “What everyone guessed” page.

Right before the answer is revealed you can optionally give feedback: how difficult the round was and, if you like, a short comment. It is stored anonymously together with the day and only read by the person running Echoes to improve the game – please don’t write your name or other personal details into it.

The statistics service runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the request. Details: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Optional account

You can sign in with your e-mail address to keep your streak and results on every device and to replay past days in the archive. Playing works fully without an account.

What we store: your e-mail address, when the account was created and last used, your results per day (the same progress your browser keeps: voices heard, guesses, points) and whether you asked for news. Sign-in codes and session keys are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; a code expires after 10 minutes.

What for: only to sign you in and to keep your progress (legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR – providing the account you asked for). If you tick “Send me occasional news about Echoes”, we may also send you short updates about the game (legal basis: your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You can untick it in your account at any time; we never pass your address on and never sell it.

Sending the code: the sign-in e-mail is delivered by Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), which processes your address and the mail for this purpose. Details: Resend Privacy Policy. The account data itself is stored with the statistics service on Cloudflare.

How long: until you delete your account. “Delete account” in the account menu removes your address and all saved results right away. You also have the right to access, correct and restrict your data and to complain to a data protection authority.

No other third parties

All scripts, the world map and the audio files are served from this site itself. Apart from the statistics service and the sign-in mail above, no fonts, analytics or other third-party services are used.

AI voices

All characters are fictional. Their voices were created in advance with ElevenLabs and are played as ordinary audio files – nothing you do in the game is sent to ElevenLabs.

Images

The night view of the Earth on the globe is “Black Marble 2016” by NASA Earth Observatory, the day view when zooming in is NASA’s “Blue Marble Next Generation” (both public domain) – all served from this site. Country borders: Natural Earth.
