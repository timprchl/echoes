Privacy

Echoes is a free hobby project. It has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no tracking. The only thing it collects is an anonymous summary of each finished round, so everyone can see what others guessed.

Who is responsible

Tim Pröchel · contact via GitHub: github.com/timprchl

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

What is stored on your device

Your progress for the day, your streak and which tips you have already seen are saved in your browser’s local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Anonymous game statistics

When you finish a round, the game sends a short, anonymous summary to our statistics service: the day, how many voices you heard, on which guess you found it, your points, whether you named the event, and for each guess only the country and the decade. To count each device once per day it uses a random ID created in your browser – no name, no account, no IP address is stored. These summaries are shown to all players as percentages on the “What everyone guessed” page.

Right before the answer is revealed you can optionally give feedback: how difficult the round was and, if you like, a short comment. It is stored anonymously together with the day and only read by the person running Echoes to improve the game – please don’t write your name or other personal details into it.

The statistics service runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the request. Details: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

No other third parties

All scripts, the world map and the audio files are served from this site itself. Apart from the statistics service above, no fonts, analytics or other third-party services are used.

AI voices

All characters are fictional. Their voices were created in advance with ElevenLabs and are played as ordinary audio files – nothing you do in the game is sent to ElevenLabs.

Images

The night view of the Earth on the globe is “Black Marble 2016” by NASA Earth Observatory, the day view when zooming in is NASA’s “Blue Marble Next Generation” (both public domain) – all served from this site. Country borders: Natural Earth.
